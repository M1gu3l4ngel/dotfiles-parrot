# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of the project, and to
anyone who represents it elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Respect for every person, whatever their experience, origin, identity or opinions.
- Feedback about the work, never about the person, and the willingness to accept it.
- Responsibility for our mistakes, and an apology to whoever they affected.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, and sexual or discriminatory language or images.
- Personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Report a problem

Write to the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub profile, with the subject
`Code of conduct: dotfiles-parrot`. Every report is reviewed and answered, and the identity of
whoever reports is kept private.

## Consequences

The maintainer decides, in proportion to what happened, between a private warning, a public
warning, a temporary ban and a permanent ban, as the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe.
